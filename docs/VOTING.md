# NIGHT SHIFT — voting-month playbook (Dec 1 → early Jan)

itch is a forum with executables attached. Voting month is won in the
comments, collections, and feeds — not just on the game page. Cadence:
~30 min/day. Everything below is free.

## Daily ops (the loop)

1. **Play + rate 3–5 entries/day**, deduction/puzzle/mystery tags first
   (reciprocal audience), then anything with <10 ratings (they remember).
2. **Comment with the quality bar:** cite ONE specific mechanic by name
   ("the way your X recontextualizes Y"), one honest friction, one
   question. Never generic praise — devs can smell it and voters read
   your comments on your own page.
3. **Reply to every comment on NIGHT SHIFT within 24h.** Answer design
   questions with numbers (cite the audit). Thank raters by referring to
   something in *their* game — this is the reciprocity engine.
4. **One devlog per week** (drafts in `docs/DEVLOG_DRAFTS.md` + results
   post). Devlogs notify followers and surface in feeds.

## Rating log

than rating high — jam voters compare calibration.

## Collections outreach (week 1)

Ask 3 curators of deduction/puzzle/jam collections to include NIGHT SHIFT.
Message template: one sentence what it is, one sentence why it fits *their*
collection ("saw your [collection] — NIGHT SHIFT is a 5-day deduction
game about enforcing rules you deduced; the finale rule is player-written,
which pairs with [their X]"). No follow-ups. If declined, thank and move on.

## Share threads (weekly, not daily)

Post the daily-docket grid + `?seed=` replay link in the jam's discussion
and one deduction-game thread. Format: grid, one-line pitch, one question
("day 3's liar got me — did anyone spot it blind?"). Questions pull replies;
replies pull plays. Never post the same seed twice — spoilers rot trust.

## Don't

- Don't ask for ratings directly, ever. Play, comment well, and let the
  log do the asking.
- Don't rate entries you haven't finished (or at least genuinely played).
  The community is small and remembers.
- Don't patch the jam build mid-voting except for crashes/blockers. Log
  everything else for the post-jam milestone — voters distrust a moving
  target, and the patch-notes devlog becomes content.
