## World Store — long

Last Human Standing is a daily real-world elimination game built for verified humans. No bots, no random chance—just a brutal test of who shows up. The rules are simple:
1. The Theme: Each day, a new real-world theme drops (e.g., "AT A CAFÉ" or "A SUNRISE").
2. The Proof: Snap a photo matching the theme from anywhere on Earth.
3. The Audit: The community votes HUMAN or SUS on your proof.
4. The Elimination: The cap shrinks every day. Fail the audit or miss the window, and you're out.
5. The Prize: The last human standing takes the entire on-chain prize pool.

Built exclusively for World App, Last Human Standing uses World ID to ensure one human, one slot. Reserve your place in the next cohort, lock in your entry, and prove you're still here. One remains. Will it be you?
